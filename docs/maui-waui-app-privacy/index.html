Maui Waui App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This privacy policy explains how information is handled in the Maui Waui Festival app. The app helps festival-goers view festival information, plan their weekend, receive operational updates, and, where approved, manage artist or trader profile content.

Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to people using the Maui Waui Festival mobile app. It covers public users, festival staff, artists, traders, and approved content editors who use the app's restricted admin or portal tools.

Information We Collect

The app may collect or store the following information:

  • Anonymous app analytics, such as screens viewed, saved acts, feature use, performance timings, timestamps, and an anonymous device ID.
  • Short-lived device presence pings so the festival team can see approximate live app usage and load.
  • Email addresses entered to create a free app account and unlock enhanced features such as cards, photos, saved favourites, reminders, and alerts.
  • Optional marketing consent, if you choose to receive Maui Waui Festival news, offers, or future event updates by email.
  • Push notification tokens used to send festival alerts and saved-set reminder notifications.
  • Feedback submitted after the event, including ratings, favourite acts, and free-text comments.
  • Allowlisted sign-in information for staff, artists, and traders, including email address, session token, role, and permissions.
  • Artist and trader profile content added by approved users, such as profile text, menu lines, tags, links, photos, and moderation status.
  • Moderation records showing what approved moderators removed, when, and why.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Provide the festival schedule, artist, trader, area, live update, and saved-plan features.
  • Unlock enhanced app features for free account users who provide an email address.
  • Send important operational notifications and optional set reminders.
  • Send marketing emails only where you have actively opted in.
  • Understand app usage, capacity, reliability, and performance.
  • Improve future festival apps, event planning, and festival operations.
  • Allow approved staff, artists, and traders to sign in and manage authorised content.
  • Review and moderate user-submitted artist and trader profile content.
  • Collect post-event feedback.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use app data for third-party advertising tracking.
  • We do not require marketing consent to create a free app account or unlock app features.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not use Face ID or biometric data on our servers. If biometric confirmation is used, it is handled locally by the device.

Legal Basis And Consent

Where required, we rely on consent, legitimate interests, or contract/permitted event operations to process app information. For example, analytics and operational metrics help us run and improve the app, while approved portal accounts are needed to provide artist, trader, and admin features.

A free app account email is used to unlock enhanced app features. Marketing emails are optional and require a separate opt-in. You can use the enhanced app features without choosing to receive marketing emails.

Storage And Security

Some information is stored locally on your device so the app can keep working with poor signal. Server-side data is stored using Cloudflare services. We use role-based access checks for restricted features and keep admin, artist, and trader tools behind allowlisted sign-in.

Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you remove the app or clear its storage. Server-side operational data is kept only for as long as needed for festival operations, analytics, feedback, moderation, and future planning, unless a longer retention period is required for legal or operational reasons.

You can permanently delete a free app account in the app at any time. Open Info, choose Delete account, then enter the one-time confirmation code sent to the account email address. This deletes the stored email address, optional marketing preference, and related account record from our server, and clears the saved plan from that device. Anonymous, aggregated analytics are not linked to the free app account.

Your Choices

  • You can disable push notifications in your device settings.
  • You can remove saved-plan data by clearing the app's local storage or uninstalling the app.
  • You can choose not to opt into marketing emails when creating a free app account.
  • If you opt into marketing emails, you can unsubscribe or ask us to remove your email from marketing lists.
  • You can permanently delete your free app account in the app: Info > Delete account. We send a one-time confirmation code to protect against accidental or unauthorised deletion.
  • You can choose not to submit post-event feedback.
  • Approved portal users can ask for profile content to be corrected or removed.

Children

The app is intended as an event companion. It is not designed to collect information from children directly. Families and guardians should supervise app use by children at the festival.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or data requests, contact the festival team through https://www.mauiwauievents.co.uk/contact.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy if the app changes or if legal, operational, or store requirements change. The latest version will be made available at the privacy policy URL used in the app store listings.
