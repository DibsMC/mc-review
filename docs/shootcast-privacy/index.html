ShootCast Privacy Notice

Last updated: 29 July 2026 · Version 2026-07-29

This notice explains how Dibs McCallum uses personal data in ShootCast.

Data we process

  • Account data, including email address, display name, account status, subscription entitlement and contributor points.
  • Profile details you choose to add, such as your home area and photography interests.
  • Saved guides, private trips, group planning activity, alerts, contributions, freshness checks, messages, reports and reward-redemption records.
  • Photos and location-guide information you submit, together with photo-rights consent and moderation history.
  • Notification device tokens when you enable push notifications.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate, diagnose and protect the service.

Children

ShootCast is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. A young person who is old enough to use the service should involve a parent or guardian in decisions about sharing photographs, profile information or precise locations.

Precise location

ShootCast asks for foreground location permission only when you use a location-based feature. Your device coordinates are used to find nearby guides, resolve a local forecast and help you select a trip starting point. The current app does not continuously track you in the background. You can deny or revoke permission in device settings and choose a place manually instead.

Why we use data

We process data to provide your account and requested app features, perform our contract with subscribers, keep the service secure, moderate community content, improve reliability with proportionate diagnostics, and meet legal obligations. Where consent is the appropriate basis, such as optional device permissions and notifications, you can withdraw it through your device or app settings.

Maps and third parties

Map rendering is provided by Apple Maps on iOS and Google Maps on Android. Using Google Maps features is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. ShootCast guide descriptions, parking notes, safety notes and community contributions are maintained independently and are not represented as Google content.

We also use service providers for account authentication, infrastructure, weather forecasts, app distribution, notifications and subscriptions. Each receives only the data needed for its role and is subject to its own terms and safeguards.

Sharing and international processing

Public guide contributions and profile attribution are visible to other users. Private trips and groups are visible only to their participants unless a participant creates a share link. Service providers may process data outside the UK; appropriate contractual and organisational safeguards must be maintained where required.

Retention and your rights

We keep account and activity data only for as long as needed for the service, safety, dispute handling and legal obligations. Approved guide contributions and moderation records may be retained after account deletion in anonymised form to preserve the shared catalogue and safety history.

You can export your account data and request account deletion from Profile > Settings. Depending on applicable law, you may also ask for access, correction, restriction, objection or portability, and may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Contact

The data controller is Dibs McCallum. Privacy enquiries: hello@dibsmcmedia.com.

This notice applies to the ShootCast mobile app and its related service.
