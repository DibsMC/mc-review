Delete your ShootCast account

Last updated: 3 August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of a ShootCast account and its associated personal data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open ShootCast and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the Profile tab, then open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Select Confirm delete to complete the request.

The deletion request is processed when you confirm it. You are then signed out.

Data deleted or anonymised

We delete or anonymise personal account data associated with the account, including:

  • email sign-in credential and account access;
  • display name, account status, membership entitlement, points and blocked-member list;
  • push-notification token and notification content;
  • private saved collections, alerts, solo trips, invitations and personal itinerary details;
  • profile attribution on group activity, comments, suggestions, polls and feedback.

Data that may be retained without your identity

To protect other users and preserve the shared photography-guide catalogue, approved guide contributions, associated photos, moderation records, safety reports and limited product-feedback records may be retained after account deletion. These records are retained without your display-name identity. Content that is no longer needed for service operation, safety, dispute handling or legal obligations is deleted or anonymised.

Retention timing

There is no additional waiting period for the account-deletion action. Anonymised records are retained only for as long as needed for the purposes above; retention is not used to restore your account or re-identify you.

Need help?

If you cannot sign in, email hello@dibsmcmedia.com from the address linked to your ShootCast account. We may need to verify that the request is from the account holder before acting on it.

For further information about how ShootCast handles personal data, read the ShootCast Privacy Notice.
